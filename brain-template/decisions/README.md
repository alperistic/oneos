# decisions/

Decision records with lasting rationale — not just what was decided, but why, and what the alternative would have been. This is the place to look before someone (human or agent) tries to "fix" something that was actually a deliberate trade-off.

A minimal decision record:

```yaml
---
title: "Decision — <short description>"
created: YYYY-MM-DD
type: decision
status: active
---
```

Followed by: the decision, the reasoning, the alternatives considered, and what would have to change for this decision to be revisited.

See [../RESOLVER.md](../RESOLVER.md) for when something belongs here versus in a project note.
